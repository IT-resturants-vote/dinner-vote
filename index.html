訂位注意事項Booking notes

投票VOTING

大家投完就截止Voting closes once everyone has voted

  • 每人投 1 票，得票最高的那家就是這次聚餐地點。
  • Everyone gets 1 vote. The restaurant with the most votes wins.
  • 同票時由主辦人決定；得票最高的訂不到，就改訂第二名。
  • The organizer breaks ties. If the winner can't take the booking, we go with second place.
訂位BOOKING

28 人要提早訂Book early for 28 people

  • 饗饗、旭集是熱門吃到飽，通常要提前一個月以上訂位，28 人可能要分成好幾桌或問團體訂位。
  • INPARADISE and Sunrise are popular buffets and usually need booking a month or more ahead. 28 people may need several tables or a group booking.
  • 182 手做料理是每桌 10,000＋10%（11,000 元），28 人大約要 3 桌，每桌坐幾人會影響每人金額，訂位時問清楚一桌最多幾位。
  • 182 charges NT$10,000 + 10% (NT$11,000) per table. 28 people need about 3 tables, so the per-person cost depends on how many sit at each. Ask the maximum per table when booking.
  • 喜來登優廚是週三到週日才有午晚餐，週一、週二不能訂。
  • Sheraton U Kitchen serves lunch and dinner Wednesday to Sunday only. Monday and Tuesday aren't available.
  • 所有價格訂位時都再確認一次，網路上的價格可能已經調整。
  • Confirm prices when booking. Prices found online may have changed.
交通GETTING THERE

饗饗在台北INPARADISE is in Taipei

  • 饗饗在台北信義區微風信義 46 樓，離桃園比較遠，選這家的話要安排交通，例如包車或搭高鐵、捷運。
  • INPARADISE is on the 46th floor of Breeze Xinyi in Taipei, far from Taoyuan. If it wins we need transport, such as a chartered bus or the HSR and MRT.
  • 其他四家都在桃園，可以開車或共乘。
  • The other four are in Taoyuan and easy to reach by car or carpool.

聚餐規劃Dinner plan

第 1–3 天 · 投票DAYS 1–3 · VOTING

5 人都投完就截止Voting closes once all 5 have voted

  • 每人投 2 票給不同餐廳，想推薦別家可以先新增再投。
  • Everyone casts 2 votes for different restaurants. To suggest another place, add it first, then vote.
  • 同票時，先比較每人預估金額較低的那家；還是平手就由主辦人決定。
  • On a tie, the cheaper place per person wins. If still tied, the organizer decides.
  • 得票最高的餐廳若訂不到，改訂第二名。
  • If the winner can't take the booking, we go with second place.
投票結束當天 · 訂位AFTER VOTING · BOOKING

打電話訂 5 位，確認價格Call to book for 5 and confirm prices

  • 訂位時再問一次目前價格和服務費，網路上的價格可能已調整。
  • Ask for the current price and service charge when booking. Prices found online may have changed.
  • 鐵板燒：3 位以上要先付訂金 1,000 元，只收現金，當天可折抵。
  • Teppanyaki: groups of 3+ pay a NT$1,000 cash deposit, deducted from the bill.
  • 燒肉：訂位時問清楚會長盛合的價格和是否收服務費，5 人總額抓 7,000 元內。
  • Yakiniku: confirm the platter price and service charge. Keep the total for 5 under NT$7,000.
  • 自助餐：只訂週一到週四晚餐，週五晚上和週末會超過預算。
  • Buffet: book a Monday–Thursday dinner only. Friday dinner and weekends go over budget.
聚餐前 · 交通與預算BEFORE DINNER · TRANSPORT AND BUDGET

先講好集合方式和點餐上限Agree on meeting point and spending cap

  • 5 人總預算 7,500 元（含服務費）。燒肉要現場加點，點餐時以這個總額為上限。
  • Total budget for 5 is NT$7,500 including service charge. Yakiniku is ordered at the table, so keep to that total.
  • 飲料、酒水也算在 1,500 元內，要額外加點請自費並事先說好。
  • Drinks count toward the NT$1,500. Anything extra is paid personally and agreed in advance.
  • 從新屋出發到高鐵站一帶建議共乘，停車場與集合時間先在群組確認。
  • Carpool from Xinwu to the HSR area. Confirm parking and meeting time in the group chat.
聚餐當天ON THE DAY

吃完結帳就散會Pay and head home after dinner

  • 不安排續攤和其他活動，吃完就各自回家，整頓飯大約 1.5 小時。
  • No second round or other activities. Dinner takes about 1.5 hours, then everyone heads home.
  • 由一人統一結帳，收據拍照給大家，確認每人沒有超過 1,500 元。
  • One person pays and shares a photo of the receipt to confirm nobody went over NT$1,500.
